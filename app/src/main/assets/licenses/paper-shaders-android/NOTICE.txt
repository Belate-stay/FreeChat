Paper Shaders Android / Jetpack Compose port

This package is a native Android port of paper-design/shaders:
https://github.com/paper-design/shaders

The original Paper Shaders project is licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
Shader algorithms, presets, and visual design are based on the upstream work by
Paper.

FreeChat adapts the circular cell/fract distance and antialias mask from:
https://github.com/AndreFrelicot/paper-shaders-android
paper-shaders-compose/src/main/kotlin/dev/andrefrelicot/papershaders/shaders/DotGridAgsl.kt

FreeChat modifications: fixed dot-grid cells, coherent regional breathing,
theme-based ink and gloss, feathered rounded opacity, and an older Android /
software Canvas fallback. No noise texture or full shader library is included.
